Privacy Policy

This app stores the email address and (hashed, never plain-text) password you register with, plus whatever todo lists and items you create.

Please don't reuse a real password here — use a throwaway one, since this is a demo project, not a security-audited production service.

Registration is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process your IP address as part of verifying you're not a bot — see Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that.

Data isn't sold or shared with any other third party. Everything here may be deleted at any time.
