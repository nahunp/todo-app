Privacy Policy

This app stores the email address you register with in plain text, and your password as a one-way hash — never in a form that could be reversed to see the real password, not even by us. The database itself is encrypted at rest and in transit (standard protections on the Azure SQL database this runs on), but individual fields like the email aren't separately encrypted on top of that — the same as almost every login system, since the app needs to look accounts up by email to let you sign in. Also stored: whatever todo lists and items you create.

Please don't reuse a real password here — use a throwaway one, since this is a demo project, not a security-audited production service.

Registration is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process your IP address as part of verifying you're not a bot — see Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that.

Data isn't sold or shared with any other third party. Everything here may be deleted at any time.
